Glass gauntlet strategy and archive review — 2026-10-03

This is a static, read-only prebattle review. No game input, save alteration, live battle log, AI choice or RNG inspection was used. The baseline is Glass-10AM-paused-status.json; later normal preparation/checkpoints take precedence. Installed Reborn19.5.43 takes precedence over the walkthrough.

Critical correction after source/UI review: Xitems affect only the Pokémon whose action opens the bag. Arcanine cannot use an Xitem on Klinklang. Battle_Scene.pbItemMenu3359 sets retindex=index for battleitems; Battle.pbRegisterItem1998 registers that actor's item action. Ordinary Xitems do not skip the allied partner's action: the skip is only for items having the separate UseInBattle handler, such as balls. Potions/Full Heal/Revive have the party target menu, so the partner can heal an ally. All plans below use legal self-Xitems. The earlier proposed allied-Xitem openers are superseded.

Exact Factory mechanics

Installed fieldtext.rb971–1023 gives Gear Grind2× damage and Electric1.5×. Fire has no general Factory bonus. Battle_MoveEffects.rb1643 gives Shift Gear +2Attack/+2Speed on Factory. X Defend/X Sp.Def/X Sp.Atk/X Speed each give two stages in this build (ItemEffects.rb3279 onward).

Avoid unnecessary Earthquake/Discharge: they change Factory into Short Circuit. Short Circuit gives Gear Grind1.5× and changes it to Electric, so Ground types can become immune; its Synthetic Seed also differs. A Factory Synthetic Seed gives+1Sp.Atk and Laser Focus. Vivillon, Mismagius and Jynx can therefore land dangerous critical hits if allowed a damaging turn.

Wide Lens does not make Gear Grind certain: the installed accuracy formula floors85×1.1 to 93%. Gear Grind makes one accuracy check for the two-hit move, not one for each hit. Twelve uses at93% have only~42% probability of no misses. Reserve the two owned X Accuracy for Klinklang in rounds1 and3. X Accuracy's+2 accuracy stages exceeds100% even after Snow Cloak's0.8 hail modifier, unless additional unreviewed evasion effects intervene.

Cofagrigus's Trick Room lasts five turns on Factory with Spell Tag. Eight turns require the installed Psychic Terrain/Chess/New World condition or an Amplifield Rock. Do not combine Trick Room casually with a fast boosted Klinklang; reserve it for a slower backup plan if the sweep collapses.

Round1: Arcanine supports Klinklang

Suggested opening, adapting if an actual critical hit/target change alters HP:

  1. Arcanine self-XSpeed; Klinklang self-XDefend. Both items occur before attacks, and each partner still has its own action. Arcanine's entry Intimidate reduces Marowak's ordinary physical damage.
  2. Arcanine now352Speed uses Flamethrower on Ribombee before its310Speed action; Klinklang uses Shift Gear. Flame still KOs through Light Screen226–265 vs214HP. If Arcanine is at121HP or less after turn1, use Klinklang's action to Ultra Potion Arcanine, delaying Shift Gear: Marowak Shadow Bone can do120.
  3. Arcanine's fast Flamethrower eliminates Vivillon through Light Screen (295–347 vs243HP), preventing Sleep Powder/seed critical Hurricane. Klinklang heals itself if at102HP or less; otherwise it can self-XAccuracy, or take the delayed Shift Gear when actually safe. Do not force a nonheal action while another ordinary Marowak Fire Punch can KO it.
  4. Heal as needed from Arcanine's action; boosted Klinklang removes Marowak. Finish Klinklang's self-XAccuracy at a safe opportunity if not already used.

This order intentionally keeps Marowak alive while the two first Shelly Pokémon are removed; therefore Meowstic cannot yet enter with Rain Dance. It also lets fast Arcanine eliminate Vivillon itself, freeing Klinklang's action for healing or setup. Exact HP and any unexpected switch still take precedence.

Klinklang's+2Speed is374. Ribombee is310, Meowstic264, Vivillon222 and Nidoking224. Arcanine starts176; its own X Speed produces352, but Sticky Web can alter that round1 stage calculation. Clear Body protects Klinklang from Web/Icy Wind stat loss. Scolipede262 becomes393 after one Speed Boost; target it promptly or give Klinklang another X Speed/Shift Gear before it gains too much momentum.

The primary risk is Marowak's critical Fire Punch: it ignores Intimidate and positive Defense and does~383–450 to Klinklang275HP. A round1 Klinklang faint does not invalidate the full chain because the scripted full heal revives it. Healthy Swampert's Waterfall and Excadrill's Rock Slide are the recovery tools; Mienshao's Wide Guard protects spread attacks. Use Earthquake only if its immediate benefit outweighs abandoning Factory.

For ordinary hits, Ribombee's Pollen Puff can do62 to Klinklang, exceeding its spread Dazzling Gleam42. With Marowak Fire Punch102, Klinklang can be as low as111 after turn1 and9 after turn2; the turn3 self-heal is essential in that case. Arcanine can be109 after turn1 from Shadow Bone120 plus Pollen Puff65; the conditional turn2 allied potion above prevents a second Bone knockout. These bounds do not protect against critical hits or an unexpected move effect.

Meowstic can use Rain Dance/Reflect. Do not assume Arcanine still one-shots Ribombee through rain plus Light Screen, and avoid re-entering Intimidate against Competitive Meowstic without accounting for the special-attack boost. Prioritize dangerous Fire/Ground users and Sleep Powder with Klinklang; use Arcanine's spare turns for healing/buffs. Primarina's Water Hyper Voice, Nidoking's Earth Power and Volcarona's Heat Wave demand actual HP/target review.

Round2: safe special Arcanine setup

Weavile has Icicle Crash/Poison Jab/Brick Break/Night Slash, no Low Kick or Superpower. Use it as the setup opportunity, preserving the other five.

Recommended order: X Defend, X Speed early, second X Defend, two X Sp.Def, then three X Sp.Atk, inserting healing/status cures when needed. Do not boost Attack: Gothitelle and Umbreon have Foul Play. One X Speed makes Arcanine352, above Weavile319, Mismagius286 and Mega Absol308. Before that speed boost, Weavile moves before Morning Sun; use a priority bag heal if HP is threatened.

Keep Arcanine roughly180HP or higher before a nonhealing setup action. Night Slash's critical hit can do126–149 even after Defense boosts; Poison Jab can add poison chip. Ultra Potion restores 200HP, not a full heal. After the speed boost, Morning Sun normally restores147HP before Weavile attacks. A status cure alone can still leave Arcanine in range of the next attack; handle HP before poison if necessary.

At+6Sp.Atk, Charcoal Flamethrower KOs every unboosted target. Weavile's Focus Sash needs a second hit; Extreme Speed can finish1HP without allowing another ordinary attack. Mismagius must be outsped and KOed before its seed-focused critical hit. Umbreon's damage margin is narrow: 344–405 vs340HP, so verify Charcoal has not been lost and do not casually lower the special setup. Protect consumes PP but24 starting Flamethrower PP affords room for the entire round and a useful round3 reserve. All enemy trainer bags in the reviewed three rounds are null.

Round3: rebuild Klinklang, retain living party and PP

Stat stages reset between battles. Luna's Arcanine boosts do not carry into round3. The opponents lead Clefable and Alolan Ninetales; Ninetales starts hail.

Suggested first turn: Arcanine self-XSpeed; Klinklang self-XSp.Def. Clefable's Fire Blast is special: X Defend is the wrong opener protection. Without a boost it does163–192 to Klinklang; X Sp.Def reduces that to83–98.

Turn2: fast Arcanine Flamethrower Ninetales; Klinklang Shift Gear. If Nine used Veil on turn1, Flame does180–212 and leaves10–42HP, but Nine caused no turn1 damage. If Nine attacked on turn1, no Veil and fast Flame KOs before its next attack. Under those ordinary-hit branches, Klinklang survives two turns with at least20HP. On turn3, Arcanine uses Ultra Potion on Klinklang while Klinklang Gear Grind removes a surviving Ninetales, or Clefable if Ninetales is already gone. If actual paralysis interrupts the setup, cure it with the partner's Full Heal before continuing. Do not let theoretical turn numbers override the screen.

Once Sandslash is present, Arcanine's352Speed narrowly exceeds Slush Rush350; Flamethrower is4× effective and can eliminate it while Klinklang attacks the other lead. After the leads are cleared, Klinklang can self-XDefend for the physical replacements, then self-XAccuracy. Arcanine spends those setup turns healing it or removing an urgent threat. Keep a potion reserve; both buffs consume Klinklang's own action and cannot accompany its Gear Grind.

Then eliminate Clefable before repeated Fire Blast/Thunder Wave. Cofagrigus cannot stop Thunder Wave aimed at Klinklang; Full Heal is an ordinary emergency option. Brick Break removes Aurora Veil when Mienshao can enter safely. Wide Guard blocks Blizzard/Dazzling Gleam/Rock Slide/Hyper Voice, but not Psychic, Fire Blast, Freeze-Dry, Thunderbolt or a Z-move's full residual damage.

Boosted Gear Grind handles most foes even through Veil. Bronzong, Bisharp and Metagross resist Steel; use Arcanine's Flamethrower and/or a second Shift Gear. Arcanine is slower than Slush Rush Sandslash350 without its own speed help; Klinklang374 outruns it after one Shift Gear. A second Shift Gear produces Klinklang561Speed and+4Attack on Factory. Do not re-enter Arcanine casually against Defiant Bisharp: Intimidate triggers its boost. Save a healthy Cofagrigus/Swampert/Excadrill recovery option if the main pair loses momentum.

Alakazam can replace Factory with Psychic Terrain; stop assuming Factory damage after that occurs. Jynx's Synthetic Seed is dangerous if Factory is still active. Froslass has Destiny Bond; boosted faster Klinklang can normally KO it before the initial Bond, but observe whether a previous Bond is active before attacking. After a Bond, let it expire through a safe nondamaging turn or accept the final-round trade only with enough surviving teammates.
