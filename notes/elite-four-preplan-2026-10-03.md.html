Gravity is a real installed exception: Battle_MoveEffects.rb:7996 sets5turns (8 with Rock), Battle_Field.rb:473–522 applies the NewWorld fieldChange:STARLIGHT rule, and Battle.rb:5916 restores NewWorld when Gravity ends. This removes the quarter-EQ penalty and NewWorld's Judgment/EarthPower boosts temporarily and stops the NewWorld type-roll behavior while the field remains Starlight, but Starlight still boosts DoomDesire×4 and adds Fire. Dark attacks also acquire Starlight's Fairy matchup addon. This is a field-management option, not immunity to the strongest hit. Do not refresh Gravity while already active (it fails), forget its timer, or claim ordinary MistyTerrain works first. Geomancy is the other source-listed field changer; no selected owned Pokémon is assumed to have it. This differs from the guide's blanket assertion that field change is unavailable. BigJRA's League section remains a useful overview; installed handlers take precedence.

Status insurance is legal, not automatically a guaranteed one-turn kill. The ordinary trainer PULSEform is not an isbossmon shield opponent. Moonlight/FullRestore mean more than four calendar turns may be required. Safely deliver Curse/Bond with a genuine free entry, reviewed HP/speed, intact Sash and no pending second hit. An entry flinch can prevent the move. Keep another living member throughout. Do not enqueue a promised win based on enemy move selection.
