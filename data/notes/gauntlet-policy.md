# Multi-battle gauntlets

The user requires every round of a gauntlet to be won consecutively. This applies to Fiore and every later multi-battle sequence.

Before entering, inspect all installed opponent and partner rosters, fields, scripted healing, item carryover, and opportunities to change the party. Plan one team and a consumable budget for the entire sequence. The installed version takes precedence over older guides.

Do not save or reload between its rounds. A reset starts a new whole-chain attempt. Ordinary automatic healing is part of the sequence. Use fresh screen observations and normal game inputs; never read live AI/RNG or alter game/save data.

Keep every attempt, including failures and resets, in the full journey. For the major-fights video, retain only victories from the final completed chain. Hold intermediate victories unpublished until the final round is won, and mark their attempt records with `gauntlet_completed: true` only then. Preserve their source footage while the chain is pending.

Recording rotation may happen at an inter-round dialogue pause without changing game state. Record again before sending any further game input. Verify the final world checkpoint before claiming completion.
